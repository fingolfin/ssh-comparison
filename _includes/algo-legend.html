{%- comment -%} Explains the colour coding of the id column in words, so the status is readable without colour. {%- endcomment -%}
  • secure — no known weakness
  • weak — deprecated, or attackable only in theory or at impractical cost
  • broken — practically attackable today
  • no status — not rated here

Hover over an algorithm for the reason. The evidence behind the ratings is on the attacks page.
